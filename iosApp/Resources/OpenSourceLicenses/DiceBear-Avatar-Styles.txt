Silo's profile avatar presets are generated by the DiceBear HTTP API
(https://www.dicebear.com) when they are displayed. The artwork is not
bundled with the app. Fun Emoji and Bottts are remixes of the original works
credited below; the other styles are DiceBear's own.

Fun Emoji
  A remix of "Fun Emoji Set" by Davis Uche
  (https://www.figma.com/community/file/968125295144990435),
  licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Bottts
  Silo uses the "bottts-neutral" style, a remix of "Bottts" by Pablo Stanley
  (https://bottts.com/), free for personal and commercial use.

Pixel Art
  Silo uses the "pixel-art-neutral" style by DiceBear, licensed under
  CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Identicon and Initials
  By DiceBear, licensed under CC0 1.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
